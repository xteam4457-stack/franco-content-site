Internal creator workflow

AI-assisted content discovery and planning.

Franco Content Agent is an internal tool used to discover visual ideas, organize content candidates, evaluate creative fit, and prepare content recommendations for human review.

Content discovery

Finds relevant visual ideas and content references based on predefined lifestyle and creator themes.

AI-assisted review

Analyzes candidate content for relevance, visual quality, timing, category fit, and duplication risk.

Human approval

Final recommendations are reviewed by a human before anything is approved for publishing.

Pinterest data, where available through authorized API access, is used for discovery, ranking, trend research, and creative inspiration. Pinterest media is not automatically republished outside Pinterest by this application.
