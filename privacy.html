Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 24, 2026
This Privacy Policy explains how Franco Content Agent handles information when used as an internal AI-assisted content discovery and planning tool.

1. Purpose of the application

Franco Content Agent helps an authorized creator/business workflow discover, organize, evaluate, and recommend content ideas and visual references. Final publishing decisions are made by a human reviewer.

2. Information the application may process

Depending on the connected services and permissions granted, the application may process:

  • basic account and application identifiers required for authentication;
  • public Pin, board, and related metadata made available through authorized Pinterest API access;
  • content URLs, titles, descriptions, categories, and media metadata used for discovery and ranking;
  • internal review status, scheduling data, and content-selection records;
  • technical logs needed to operate and troubleshoot the application.

3. How information is used

Information is used only to:

  • discover relevant content ideas and trends;
  • rank and categorize visual references;
  • detect duplicates and improve recommendation quality;
  • prepare content candidates for human review;
  • maintain the security and reliability of the application.

4. Pinterest content

Pinterest information is used for discovery, analysis, ranking, and creative inspiration where access is provided through authorized Pinterest API permissions. The application does not automatically republish Pinterest media outside Pinterest.

5. Authentication and credentials

API keys, access tokens, and service credentials are stored in private environment secrets or equivalent protected configuration and are not intentionally exposed through the public website.

6. Sharing and sale of data

Franco Content Agent does not sell personal information. Data is not shared with unrelated third parties for advertising or data-broker purposes. Information may be processed by service providers strictly as needed to operate the workflow.

7. Data retention

Operational data is retained only for as long as reasonably necessary for content discovery, review, scheduling, troubleshooting, and security. Records may be deleted when they are no longer required.

8. Security

Reasonable technical measures are used to protect credentials and operational data, including private repositories, access controls, and secret-management features provided by connected services.

9. Your choices

Authorized users may revoke connected-service permissions through the relevant service account settings. Revoking access may prevent the application from using that service.

10. Contact

Privacy inquiries may be directed to the application owner using the business contact details associated with the connected Pinterest Business account.
